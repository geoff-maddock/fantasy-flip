How to Play

Goal. Score the most points over 3 rounds of 5 turns by adventuring in four locations, growing your hero, and surviving the Monster Assault at the end of each round.

The Turn

Each turn, 4 cards are flipped into the Omen Row. You take 2 of them. Click a card, tweak it if you like, then click a glowing space on a board to write it in. Number cards go to the location matching their suit:

♠ Dungeon — clear rooms in order. Fights need high cards, traps need low cards.
♣ Forest — write values on the trail in strictly rising order.
♥ Temple — odd values on the Sun altar, even on the Moon; no repeats on an altar. Higher sums score more Spirit.
♦ Market — sell the card for gold (half its value, rounded up), or buy an item: card value must reach the price, and you pay the price in gold.

Face cards are events, not numbers: Jack = +3 gold · Queen = +2 mana or heal 2 wounds · King = +1 XP and −1 Threat. Aces count as 1 and grant +1 XP when written.

Any taken number card may instead be camped for 1 gold if you have no good use for it.

Board Selection

At setup you choose one board per suit from three options each — 81 combinations. Every board scores into its suit's star track (♠ Glory, ♣ Lore, ♥ Spirit, ♦ Fortune) but plays by different rules: gauntlets, rondels, blackjack musters, spatial clearing, rising sums, one-way pilgrimages, prophecies, pattern-cutting, and road networks. Mix them for a different puzzle every game.

Bending Fate

While a card is selected: pay 1 gold per ±1 to its value, or 2 mana to change its suit. Modified values count for everything (parity, thresholds, order).

Hero Hand

At the start of each round, draw 3 cards from your own deck, and draw 1 more at the start of each turn (hand limit 3). Playing a hand card is a free extra action — it doesn't use one of your 2 picks. Spend them for big turns; a full hand means wasted draws.

Threat & the Assault

Every face card left in the Omen Row at end of turn adds +1 Threat. After turn 5, monsters attack: compare your Defense (bosses slain, Shield, Temple wards, class) to the Threat. Fully defend to gain Glory and XP; otherwise take Wounds (−2 pts each, healable at the Temple or by Queens).

Spending XP — The Adventurers' Guild

XP is worth 1 point each at game end, but during play you may spend it at the Guild to charter new adventure locations (3 XP for the first, 5 for the second, 7 for the third):

🗼 Wizard's Tower — any suit: five floors built bottom-up, each value strictly lower than the one beneath.
🐊 Beast Bog — ♠/♣: bait a pen, then trap the beast with a matching value. Pens must use different values.
🍺 Gilded Tavern — ♥/♦: build a run — each new value exactly one above or below a value already played.

Difficulty

🌿 Adventurer: assault base Threat 2/4/6. ⚔ Veteran: 3/5/8, start with less gold. 💀 Nightmare: 4/7/10, no starting mana, deeper wound caps. Leftover face cards add to the base as usual.

Experimental Modules

📊 Attributes: spend a number card of the matching suit to train — value ≥ 4 for level 1, ≥ 7 for level 2, ≥ 10 for level 3. Levels grant permanent bonuses (shown on the panel).
🧑‍🌾 Camp Followers: follower icons on certain spaces recruit when you fill them; Jacks recruit a Trader; repelling an assault recruits a Squire. Spend a follower any time for its one-shot action.
✨ Spellcraft: select a first card, choose a spell, then click a second card (row cards each use a pick; hand cards are free). Both cards are consumed along with the mana. Face cards count as value 10 in spells.

🎲 Push Your Luck: after clearing a dungeon room you may Delve — flip cards one at a time; red cards bank gold equal to the room's depth (red faces add mana), one black number is a peril, a second black number or any black face is an ambush: lose the unbanked loot and take a Wound. Overdraw lets you draw extra hero cards; a face card escapes to the horde for +1 Threat. At the assault you may make a Last Stand, flipping cards for +1 Defense (red) or +1 Threat (black), stopping whenever you dare. At 5+ Wounds you are Battered: hand limit 2.

♞ The Tyrant's Gambit: a 5×5 chess puzzle open to any suit. Your card's suit sets the Champion's move — ♠ rook lines, ♥ bishop diagonals, ♦ queen lines, ♣ a knight's leap — and its value caps how far a line move travels. Lines are blocked by pieces. Land on a courtier to capture it and take its reward; the ♚ Shadow King can only be captured while no other piece attacks his square, so break his guard first. Red pips show squares the court attacks — end your turn on one and the court harries you for +1 🔥 Threat.

🜁 Runecraft: rune-marked spaces (☄️💧🌪️🪨 icons) grant their rune when you claim them — they appear on every board, the Market's Torch/Amulet/Crown, gem settings, trade towns, chess captures, true prophecies, and more. Every 2 runes of a type is one charge of its power: ☄️ Searing Sigil (+4 to the selected card), 💧 Tidecall (sweep and redraw the Omen Row), 🌪️ Galestep (+1 pick and draw a hero card), 🪨 Stoneward (+1 🛡 permanently and heal 2). Each complete set of all four types triggers a Confluence: +2⭐ Spirit, repeatable.

Scoring

Stars (⭐ = 2 pts) in Glory, Lore, Spirit, Fortune (+1⭐ per 6 gold at the end) · XP = 1 pt each · Wounds = −2 pts each (Necromancer: −1).

The Wizard's Tower (Spirit · any suit)

Build downward-binding wards: any suit may be written, but each floor's value must be strictly lower than the floor beneath it. Build bottom-up.

The Beast Bog (Glory · ♠/♣ only)

Trap beasts by writing a matching pair into a pen. Each pen's value must differ from the other pens. Completing a pen grants Glory and gold equal to half the value (rounded up).

The Gilded Tavern (Fortune · ♥/♦ only)

A game of runs: the first value is free; every later value must be exactly one higher or lower than a value already written. No repeats.
