How a turn works. Four cards flip into the Omen Row (the shared adventure deck). You take 2 per turn, click a card, optionally bend it, then click a glowing legal space to "write" it — the marks render in dry-erase-marker style since the digital board is standing in for your physical sheets. Face cards are a distinct event class (Fliptown-style): Jack = gold, Queen = mana/heal, King = XP and threat reduction. Any face card you leave in the row adds +1 Threat toward the end-of-round Monster Assault — that's your Hadrian's Wall escalating-threat pressure, but tied directly to your drafting choices, which is where the calculated-decision tension lives.
The four suit-linked mini-games (Rolling Realms structure): ♠ Dungeon is an ordered delve where fights want high cards and traps want low ones, with bosses granting Defense; ♣ Wildwood is a strictly-ascending trail (Gùgōng/Architects); ♥ Temple splits odd/even values across two altars with sum-based Spirit scoring (Euphoria); ♦ Market sells for gold or buys passive items that warp the other three games.
